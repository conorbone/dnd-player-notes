    DnD 5e Home rules

    These rules are not up-to-date, just dumped in here from 2016 or so when i had a larger group, honestly i cant remember most of these or the reasons I might re write these at some point

    Most of these are up for discussion in S0, and many can be dismissed if the party doesn't like them

    Some come from other DMs and hot-takes i/ past players have had

    Rule 0: Don't make more rules

    PC CREATION RULES¶

    • Home-brew/UA on approval and or S0

    • HP gain per level - always roll instead of taking average. Reroll 1s.

    • Level 1 racial feats, depending on the campaign

    • Always variant encumbrance (S0 discus)

    • No Mystics, period

    • No secret characters - everyone knows everyone else's race/class/background (S0 discuss)

    • INT mod becomes "points" that add/remove skill proficiency, tool proficiency, or languages; or spend three "points" to get one expertise

    • Atunement is RP related, not "what did you learn from useing X for a 1H+"

    • Milestones (DMG)

    Please avoid:¶

    gets boring in long campains, ok sometimes for the memes or if you have a character based on this (pending approval)

    • No evil characters unless approved,
      • No murder hobos outside of Evil Campaigns and One shots
      • your Evil PC must care for the party majorative of the time
      • Evil past, looking to redeem themselves (move to good, neutral alignment)
    • MinMax without RP reason
    • rouge is ok with murder
    • xXXedgeLordXXx *
    • bard seduces X
    • Cerics are always 100% good

    • wizards only use fireball

    • Bard skills that require a performance, can sometimes require IRL performance (S0 Very Optional)

    COMBAT RULES¶

    • Flanking skill checks. Meant to encourage more descriptive combat actions from players

      • D20> Target AC

    • Bloodied for NPCs

    • Massive damage (DMG p273) PC & NPC

    • Lingering injuries, can be as bad as loss of limb, eye, etc.

    • Secret death saves, rolled by DM, never fudged

    • DC 10 Medicine check on downed ally that acts as another death save. Healer feat and healer's kits still usable as normal

    • Sprint - forgo all non-movement actions in a turn to move 5x normal movement speed. Straight line only, provokes opportunity attacks, which are at advantage. Also usable by enemies.

    • "Cardic" Inspiration, replaces default Inspiration (PHB/DMG)

      • usaly at table games, (S0 disscus)

    SPELL RULES¶

    • Healing Spirit (XGE) - in-combat only

    ROLEPLAYING RULES¶

    • Insight vs. Deception, when used by one player on another, has an outcome at the target players' discretion (based on roll)

    • Discourage Insight as a lie detector

      • zone of truth if you need a lie detector (comes in magic item form)

    • players take narrative control (S0 disccus)

    EXPLORING RULES¶

    • No "taking 20"

    • STR/DEX/CON checks can be rerolled for a second attempt,

      • INT/WIS/CHA
      • blown INT roll means you don't know that information, so you can't reroll it

    • No spam perception rolls, if the party wants to roll perception, we all roll and take the highest roll

    • Must be proficient in a skill you are trying to Help another player with

    SPELL RULES¶

    • Counter-charm buff. Just cures the status instead of granting advantage. Paladins can do it

    • Stunning strike is magic when it comes to resistances, to avoid stun spam monks

    GETTING KICKED RULES¶

    I don't like kicking players, so avoid this kind of bullshit

    • If a rule is not known by player or DM, go with whats seems fair in the moment. After session, find out rule and update group. Keep it moving
